Hi! I'm Claudio, a software developer from Amsterdam. I build for the web, and I have a soft spot for the tools other developers build with.

I've been writing software since I was eight. That's when I discovered that building things is awesome, and I pretty much haven't put the keyboard down since.

Most people probably know me from my time as a core maintainer of [Inertia.js](https://inertiajs.com) and my work on [Forge](https://forge.laravel.com) at [Laravel](https://laravel.com). These days I'm building MateriaalMeester with my dad, and in January I'm joining [Fixico](https://fixico.com).

Open source is a big part of what I do. Lately most of that work goes into agentic AI tooling for Laravel, like [Guardrails](https://github.com/claudiodekker/laravel-guardrails) and [Firewatch](https://github.com/claudiodekker/firewatch).

Away from the screen you'll find me in a video game, or offline with family and friends.
